Hershey vector font glyph data
==============================

File: futural.jhf (Hershey Futural / Roman Simplex style)
Source: https://github.com/kamalmostafa/hershey-fonts

The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working at
the U. S. National Bureau of Standards. The .jhf format was created by James
Hurt. This distribution may be used for any purpose, commercial or otherwise,
provided that the acknowledgements above are distributed with the font data and
that the data is not converted into the U.S. NTIS format.

Full terms: ../../LICENSES/LicenseRef-Hershey.txt

Our converter (tools/build_hershey.py) extracts the glyph strokes into
assets/fonts/hershey.json. The GPLv2+ libhersheyfont library code is not
included or used.
